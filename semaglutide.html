Do You Qualify?

You may be eligible to file a semaglutide or GLP-1 lawsuit if you can answer yes to the following:

  • You used Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus, Mounjaro, Zepbound, or Saxenda for at least one month.
  • You were diagnosed with gastroparesis, severe gastrointestinal injury, ileus, or bowel obstruction.
  • You suffered gallbladder disease, gallstones, or required gallbladder removal after starting the drug.
  • You experienced sudden vision loss or were diagnosed with NAION while on a GLP-1 medication.
  • Your injury required hospitalization, surgery, or ongoing medical care.
