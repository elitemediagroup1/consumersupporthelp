Frequently Asked

Auto insurance, answered

Do I have to have auto insurance?

Almost every state requires drivers to carry at least liability coverage. Penalties for driving uninsured can include fines, license suspension and out-of-pocket liability if you cause a crash.

What's the difference between liability and full coverage?

Liability only covers damage you cause to others. "Full coverage" usually means liability plus collision and comprehensive, which protect your own vehicle.

How are my rates determined?

Insurers consider your driving record, vehicle make and model, location, mileage, credit profile in most states, age, and the coverage limits and deductibles you choose.

Can I lower my premium?

Often, yes. Bundling with home or renters, raising your deductible, taking a defensive driving course, or switching carriers can all reduce your monthly cost. An agent can help compare options.

Is this a free policy?

No. Auto insurance is a paid product. Premiums vary based on coverage and personal factors. We help you find competitive options — the policy itself is purchased through a licensed insurer.
