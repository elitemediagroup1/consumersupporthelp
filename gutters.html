Why Gutters Matter More Than You Think

Gutters might be the most overlooked system on your home, but they protect just about everything else. By directing rainwater away from your roof, siding, and foundation, a working gutter system prevents wood rot, basement flooding, foundation cracks, ice dams, and landscape erosion.

Common gutter problems

  • Clogged with leaves and debris — water overflows the edge
  • Sagging or pulling away from the fascia
  • Rust, holes, or cracks at seams
  • Improper slope leading to standing water
  • Downspouts that dump water near the foundation
  • Ice dams forming in winter from poor drainage

Gutter materials

Aluminum is the most common — affordable, lightweight, and rust-resistant. Steel is stronger and ideal for heavy snow regions but can rust over time. Copper is beautiful and lasts 50+ years, but is significantly pricier. Vinyl is cheapest but brittle in extreme temperatures.

Gutter guards: are they worth it?

Modern micro-mesh and reverse-curve gutter guards can dramatically reduce cleaning frequency and prevent ice dams. Quality systems often come with no-clog warranties of 10 years to lifetime, and many homeowners report eliminating annual cleaning entirely after install.

Average costs

New seamless aluminum gutters run $7 to $14 per linear foot installed (about $1,500 to $3,000 for an average home). Gutter guards add $8 to $15 per linear foot. Standard cleaning runs $150 to $400 depending on home size and stories.

When to clean or upgrade

  • At least twice a year — spring and fall
  • After major storms with heavy debris
  • If water spills over the front edge during rain
  • If you see water staining on siding or near the foundation
  • Before winter to prevent ice dams
