About Final Expense Coverage

What is final expense?

Final expense (also called burial insurance) is a type of whole-life insurance designed to cover funeral costs, medical bills, and other end-of-life expenses so your family is not left with the burden.

Who can qualify?

Most adults between roughly 50 and 80 can qualify for a simplified-issue final expense policy. Many plans don't require a medical exam — just answers to a few health questions.
