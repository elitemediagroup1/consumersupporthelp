FAQ

Common questions about final expense coverage

What is final expense insurance?

Final expense insurance is a type of whole life insurance designed to cover funeral, burial, and end-of-life expenses. Coverage amounts are typically smaller than traditional life insurance, which keeps premiums affordable.

Who qualifies?

Most plans are available to U.S. residents between ages 50 and 80 who can answer a few basic health questions. Even people with common health conditions can often qualify.

Is this free coverage?

No. Final expense is an insurance product with monthly premiums. The amount depends on your age, health, and the coverage level you select. There is no cost to check eligibility or speak with an agent.

Why do I need a checking or savings account?

Premiums are paid by automatic bank draft, which keeps the policy in good standing and prevents missed payments.

What happens after I call?

A licensed agent will verify your information, review available plans, answer your questions, and — if you choose to move forward — help you start coverage the same day.
