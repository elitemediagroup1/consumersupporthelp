New York’s pest landscape spans two distinct climate zones: the dense urban downstate region (NYC, Long Island, lower Hudson Valley) where cockroaches, bed bugs, and Norway rats dominate, and the upstate region (Buffalo, Rochester, Albany) where lake-effect winters drive mice and overwintering pests into homes for 6 months a year.

The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (NYSDEC) licenses every pest control applicator under Article 33. In New York City, the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene additionally enforces the NYC Housing Maintenance Code, which makes landlords responsible for pest control in rental units. 311 complaint data is publicly available.

Cost ranges across New York typically run $100-$300 for one-time general pest service (higher in NYC), $50-$120 monthly recurring, and $300-$1,500 for bed bug treatment depending on protocol.

New York Pest Control FAQs

Are New York pest control companies licensed?

Yes. NYSDEC licenses every applicator under Article 33. Always confirm your provider holds a current commercial applicator certificate before service.

How much does pest control cost in New York?

One-time general pest service typically runs $100-$300. Monthly recurring service is $50-$120. Bed bug treatment ranges $300-$1,500 depending on protocol and unit size.

Who pays for pest control in NYC rentals — landlord or tenant?

Under the NYC Housing Maintenance Code, landlords are responsible for keeping rental units free of pests. Tenants can file 311 complaints if landlords fail to address active infestations.
