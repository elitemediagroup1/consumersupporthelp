Buffalo’s pest dynamics are driven by extreme lake-effect winters. Freeze-thaw cycles open new mouse entry points every season as wood, masonry, and weather seals expand and contract. House mice intrusion is Buffalo’s defining pest problem from October through April, and fall exclusion work (sealing every gap larger than 1/4 inch) is the most cost-effective preventive measure homeowners can take.

The University at Buffalo (UB) area sees concentrated bed bug pressure from constant student housing turnover. Off-campus apartments and shared houses around the South Campus, North Campus, and Allentown frequently report infestations.

Carpenter ants thrive in Buffalo’s mature wooded neighborhoods (Elmwood Village, Parkside, North Buffalo) where moisture-damaged wood becomes nesting habitat. Stink bugs and box elder bugs swarm into homes every fall as overwintering pests.

How do lake-effect winters affect Buffalo pest control?

What are the best ways to keep mice out of a Buffalo home in winter?

Seal foundation cracks, install door sweeps, screen vents, and keep firewood at least 20 feet from the house. Snap traps and bait stations handle any mice that make it inside.

Why do UB students often deal with bed bugs?

High tenant turnover and shared housing increase introduction risk. Heat treatment by NYSDEC-licensed pros is the most reliable single-visit solution.

Are carpenter ants destroying my Buffalo home?

Carpenter ants nest in moisture-damaged wood and can cause structural damage over time. A licensed inspection identifies nest locations and underlying moisture sources.

How do I keep stink bugs out in fall?

Caulk gaps around windows, doors, soffits, and utility penetrations before mid-September. Vacuum any that make it inside and dispose of the bag immediately.

Do Buffalo exterminators need licenses?

Yes. NYSDEC licenses every applicator under Article 33. Always verify your provider holds a current commercial applicator certificate.

How often should Buffalo homeowners get pest control?

Quarterly service is standard, with extra fall exclusion work in September-October to seal mouse entry points before winter.
