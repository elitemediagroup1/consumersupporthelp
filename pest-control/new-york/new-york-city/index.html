New York City’s 8.3 million residents share dense housing stock that makes pest control uniquely challenging. Pre-war buildings (built before 1939) share plumbing chases, steam-heat risers, and wall cavities that let German cockroaches migrate freely between units. Building-wide coordinated treatment is the only reliable approach for these structures.

Bed bugs remain one of NYC’s most-complained-about pests. Norway rats dominate alleys, subways, and ground-floor commercial spaces. House mice exploit every gap in older walls and aging weather seals.

The NYC Housing Maintenance Code (HMC) makes landlords legally responsible for keeping rental units pest-free. Tenants can file 311 complaints, and inspection data is publicly available through NYC Open Data. The NYC Department of Health enforces these standards alongside NYSDEC’s applicator licensing requirements.

Cost ranges in NYC typically run $150-$300 for one-time general pest service, $60-$120 monthly recurring, and $400-$1,500 for bed bug treatment per unit.

NYC Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in NYC?

One-time general pest service typically costs $150-$300. Monthly recurring is $60-$120. Bed bug treatment per unit ranges $400-$1,500.

Who pays for pest control in NYC rentals?

Under the NYC Housing Maintenance Code, landlords pay. Tenants can file 311 complaints to enforce this requirement.

Why are cockroaches so common in pre-war NYC buildings?

Shared plumbing chases, steam-heat risers, and wall cavities let German cockroaches migrate between units. Building-wide treatment is essential.

How effective is bed bug heat treatment in NYC?

Whole-unit heat treatment reaches lethal temperatures (120°F+) throughout structure cavities in a single visit. It is the gold standard for NYC apartments when properly applied.

How can I check my NYC building’s pest complaint history?

NYC Open Data publishes 311 housing complaint data including pest complaints. Search by address to see historical filings.

Are Norway rats dangerous?

Norway rats can carry diseases and contaminate food. Exclusion (sealing entry points), sanitation, and bait stations form the standard control approach.

Do NYC exterminators need a license?

Yes. NYSDEC licenses all applicators under Article 33. NYC additionally enforces standards through the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene.

How often should NYC residents schedule pest control?

Monthly service is common in multifamily buildings and ground-floor commercial spaces. Quarterly is reasonable for upper-floor apartments without active infestations.
