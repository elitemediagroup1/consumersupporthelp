Why Trenton and Mercer County Face Significant Termite Pressure

Mercer County sits in USDA Termite Zone 2 — moderate to heavy eastern subterranean termite activity. Combined with Trenton's older housing stock in neighborhoods like Chambersburg and Mill Hill, this creates significant termite pressure for area homeowners. Bed bugs are a growing concern in older Trenton rental properties, while mice, rats, and cockroaches persist in older multi-unit buildings near the downtown corridor and the Delaware River.

All pest control companies in Trenton and Mercer County must hold a valid New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection (NJDEP) Pesticide Applicator License. Verify licensing before hiring any exterminator. Mercer County also has local health regulations that licensed pest control companies must comply with for multi-unit residential treatments. Always ask for proof of liability insurance and a written treatment plan with a re-service guarantee.
