LA Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Los Angeles?

One-time general pest service typically costs $85-$250. Monthly recurring service runs $40-$80. Tent fumigation for drywood termites averages $1,200-$4,500.

How do I know if I have drywood termites or subterranean termites?

Drywood termites leave small pellet-shaped frass (droppings) near infested wood and require no soil contact. Subterranean termites build mud tubes connecting soil to wood. A licensed Branch 3 inspector can confirm the species.

Are Argentine ants harmful?

Argentine ants do not bite or sting humans significantly, but their massive supercolonies displace native ant species and can overwhelm kitchens and bathrooms. Perimeter treatment combined with indoor baiting is the standard approach.

What is the best treatment for LA roof rats?

Effective roof rat control combines exclusion (sealing entry points in eaves, attics, and roof lines), tree-canopy trimming away from structures, and bait stations placed in attics and outbuildings.

Do military families get special pest control rates in LA?

Many LA pest control companies offer military and veteran discounts. Bed bug treatment in military housing is typically covered by base housing offices under quality-assurance contracts.

Is pest control safe around children and pets?

CDPR-licensed applicators use pet- and child-safe products and follow re-entry intervals. Always ask your exterminator for the product label and re-entry time.

How often should I get pest control in LA?

Most LA homeowners benefit from quarterly perimeter treatment plus an annual termite inspection. Monthly service is recommended for properties with active infestations or proximity to greenbelts.

Can I do my own LA pest control?

DIY is fine for minor surface pests, but drywood termites, German cockroaches, and bed bugs almost always require licensed professional treatment to achieve full elimination.
