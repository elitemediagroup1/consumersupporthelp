California Pest Control FAQs

Are California pest control companies licensed?

Yes. The California Department of Pesticide Regulation (CDPR) requires every applicator to hold a current license, and the Structural Pest Control Board licenses termite operators separately under Branch 1 (fumigation), Branch 2 (general pest), and Branch 3 (wood-destroying organisms).

How much does pest control cost in California?

One-time general pest service typically runs $85–$250. Recurring monthly service is $40–$80. Tent fumigation for drywood termites averages $1,200–$4,500 depending on home size and location.

Do I need a termite inspection to sell my home in California?

Most real estate transactions in California require a Wood Destroying Pests and Organisms Inspection Report (often called a Section 1 / Section 2 report) from a Branch 3-licensed inspector before closing.
