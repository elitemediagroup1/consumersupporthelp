San Diego’s pest pressure is shaped by a unique combination of factors: a year-round mild Mediterranean climate, the country’s largest concentration of active-duty military families moving in and out of base housing, and coastal canyon wildlife corridors that bring rodents, opossums, raccoons, and even coyotes into residential neighborhoods.

Bed bugs are the city’s most-reported pest in multifamily and military housing, driven by the constant cycle of PCS moves between Naval Base San Diego, Naval Base Coronado, Camp Pendleton (just north), and bases nationwide. Heat treatment and chemical protocols by CDPR-licensed pros are the standard solutions.

Drywood termites concentrate in older coastal neighborhoods (La Jolla, Point Loma, Ocean Beach, Pacific Beach). Subterranean termites are widespread inland. Canyon-edge homes in Mission Hills, Hillcrest, Mission Valley, and Tierrasanta deal with rodents and wildlife pressure year-round.

Cost ranges in San Diego typically run $85-$250 for one-time general pest, $40-$80 monthly recurring, and $1,200-$4,500 for tent fumigation.

San Diego Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in San Diego?

One-time general pest service typically costs $85-$250. Monthly recurring service runs $40-$80. Tent fumigation averages $1,200-$4,500.

Do San Diego military families get pest control coverage?

On-base housing includes pest control through base housing offices. Off-base military pay private rates but often receive military discounts from CDPR-licensed companies.

Why are bed bugs common in San Diego?

Frequent PCS moves between bases nationwide historically correlate with bed bug introductions. Heat treatment by licensed pros is the gold standard.

How do I keep canyon wildlife out of my home?

Seal eaves, vents, and crawl-space openings. Trim vegetation back from structures. Avoid leaving pet food outside overnight. Bait stations and motion-deterrents help.

Is tent fumigation safe?

Tent fumigation uses sulfuryl fluoride under licensed Branch 1 supervision with strict re-entry protocols. Once aerated and cleared, the home is safe for re-occupancy per the licensed operator’s schedule.

How often should I get pest control in San Diego?

Quarterly perimeter treatment plus annual termite inspection is standard. Canyon-edge homes often benefit from monthly service.

What are the signs of drywood termites?

Small pellet-shaped frass near baseboards, window sills, and door frames. Discarded wings near light sources after swarming season.

Can I treat bed bugs myself?

DIY bed bug treatment rarely succeeds. Licensed heat treatment reaches lethal temperatures throughout the entire structure and is the most reliable single-visit solution.
