Pennsylvania Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania pest control costs vary by region. In Philadelphia and Pittsburgh, one-time treatments run $150-400. Bed bug heat treatment averages $900-2,800. Termite treatment costs $500-2,500. Rural and suburban Pennsylvania markets tend to price lower than the major metros.

Does Pennsylvania require pest control licensing?

Yes. All structural pest control companies in Pennsylvania must be licensed by the Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture (PDA). Technicians must hold a Pesticide Applicator License in the appropriate category. Always verify licensing at the PDA website before hiring any exterminator.

What is the worst pest problem in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania is one of the worst states in the country for brown marmorated stink bugs, which were first introduced to the US in Allentown in 1996 and have since spread nationwide. Stink bugs invade homes in massive numbers in fall seeking winter shelter. Termites are also a significant problem statewide, with eastern subterranean termites found throughout all 67 Pennsylvania counties.
