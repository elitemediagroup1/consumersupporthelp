Wisconsin licenses commercial pest control applicators through the Department of Agriculture, Trade and Consumer Protection (DATCP). Ask any Wisconsin exterminator for their DATCP license number, proof of insurance, and a written treatment plan.

Wisconsin Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Wisconsin?

General pest control in Wisconsin runs $120 to $300 per treatment, with quarterly plans averaging $35 to $60 per month. Bed bug treatment ranges $800 to $2,200 and termite control $500 to $2,000. Milwaukee and Madison tend to run slightly higher than smaller Wisconsin cities, but all licensed exterminators should offer free in-home inspections.

Who licenses pest control companies in Wisconsin?

The Wisconsin Department of Agriculture, Trade and Consumer Protection (DATCP) licenses every commercial pesticide applicator and pest control business in the state. Technicians must be certified in their applicable category, and businesses must carry liability insurance. Verify any Wisconsin exterminator's DATCP credential before signing.

What are the most common pests in Wisconsin?

Bed bugs, German cockroaches, carpenter ants, Norway rats, house mice, and stink bugs are the most common pests across Wisconsin. Carpenter ants are particularly common in older wood-frame homes throughout Milwaukee, Madison, and Green Bay.

When should I schedule pest control in Wisconsin?

Spring (April–May) is the optimal time for preventative pest control in Wisconsin because overwintering insects emerge and rodents seek new nests as the weather warms. Carpenter ant swarms appear in late spring. Bed bugs and cockroaches breed indoors year-round, so treat those infestations immediately.
