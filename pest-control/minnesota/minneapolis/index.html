Why Minneapolis Is a Top 40 Bed Bug City

Minneapolis ranked #40 on Orkin's 2025 Top 50 Bed Bug Cities list — rising 7 spots from the previous year. Minneapolis Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Minneapolis?

Minneapolis pest control costs $100-265 for a standard one-time treatment. Bed bug heat treatment averages $750-2,100 for a typical Minneapolis apartment or home. Minnesota's shorter pest season means pricing is somewhat more favorable than year-round markets, though demand spikes sharply in spring and fall. Getting multiple quotes is especially important in Minneapolis where pricing varies significantly between providers.

Why is Minneapolis rising in bed bug rankings?

Minneapolis ranked #40 on Orkin's 2025 Top 50 Bed Bug Cities list, rising 7 spots from the previous year. Minneapolis's large University of Minnesota student housing market, growing apartment density in Uptown and Northeast Minneapolis, and position as a major Midwest travel hub all contribute to rising bed bug activity. Light rail transit corridors connecting the airport to downtown are also a noted bed bug vector.

Does Minnesota require pest control licensing?

Yes. All pest control companies in Minneapolis must be licensed by the Minnesota Department of Agriculture (MDA). Technicians must hold a valid Commercial Pesticide Applicator License. Verify any company's MDA license before hiring. Hennepin County also has local regulations regarding pesticide use near lakes and waterways that licensed companies must follow.

What pests are most common in Minneapolis homes?

Minneapolis homeowners most frequently encounter bed bugs in apartment buildings especially near the University of Minnesota in Dinkytown and Stadium Village, mice and rats entering homes from October through April during Minnesota's long winters, carpenter ants in older Uptown and South Minneapolis bungalows with mature tree canopy, and boxelder bugs invading exterior walls in massive numbers every September and October.

What is the best time for pest control in Minneapolis?

September is Minneapolis's most critical pest control month. Rodent exclusion work should be completed before the first hard frost — mice begin entering homes when outdoor temperatures drop below 40 degrees. Spring (April-May) is the window for carpenter ant treatment as colonies emerge from winter dormancy. Summer (June-August) is peak bed bug season with increased travel and student housing turnover near the University of Minnesota.
