Chandler Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Chandler?

Termite treatment ranges $475-2,400. Chandler's well-established neighborhoods and proximity to desert preserves make scorpion and termite pressure consistent year-round.

Why are scorpions common in Chandler?

Chandler's location in the southeast Phoenix metro places it within prime bark scorpion territory. Desert preserves near Chandler including the Gila River area maintain established scorpion populations that migrate into adjacent residential areas. New construction in south Chandler near Fulton Ranch and Santan Village regularly disturbs scorpion habitat pushing them into newer neighborhoods. Quarterly barrier treatment is the standard recommendation.

Is Chandler a high termite risk area?

Yes. Chandler is within Maricopa County's moderate to heavy termite zone. Subterranean termites are active nearly year-round in the Chandler area. The Ocotillo and Fulton Ranch areas with their water features and landscaping create moisture conditions that support termite colony activity. Annual inspections are recommended for all Chandler homeowners.

Does Arizona require pest control licensing in Chandler?

Yes. All pest control companies in Chandler must hold a valid Arizona Office of Pest Management (OPM) license. Verify at azda.gov/opm before hiring. Always confirm OPM licensing and liability insurance before any treatment begins on your property.

What pests should Chandler homeowners watch for year-round?

Chandler homeowners should monitor year-round for bark scorpions especially in garage areas, block walls, and landscaping where they hide during the day. Subterranean termites are active most of the year — watch for mud tubes along foundations and in crawl spaces. American cockroaches enter homes during monsoon season through drains and gaps. Black widow spiders are common in undisturbed areas like storage boxes, pool equipment, and block walls.
