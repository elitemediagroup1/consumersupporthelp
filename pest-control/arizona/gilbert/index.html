Gilbert Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Gilbert?

Gilbert pest control costs $75-195 for a standard one-time treatment. Scorpion barrier treatment runs $90-240 initially with quarterly follow-ups at $70-140. Termite treatment ranges $475-2,400. Gilbert's rapid growth from agricultural land to one of the fastest-growing cities in America has created significant pest displacement — farms and desert land converted to residential neighborhoods disturb established scorpion and termite colonies regularly.

Are scorpions a problem in Gilbert?

Yes. Gilbert's agricultural and desert history means scorpion populations are well-established throughout the city. As former farmland and desert has been converted to master-planned communities like Power Ranch and Morrison Ranch, scorpion activity in those neighborhoods has been consistently reported by homeowners. Newer construction areas in southeast Gilbert near the San Tan Mountains see particularly active scorpion pressure.

Does Gilbert have significant termite activity?

Yes. Gilbert's agricultural past left significant organic matter in the soil that supports subterranean termite colonies. The city's water features, irrigation systems, and lush landscaping in communities like Val Vista Lakes and Agritopia create moisture conditions that termites thrive in. Annual termite inspections are especially important for Gilbert homeowners in areas converted from agricultural use.

Does Arizona require pest control licensing in Gilbert?

Yes. Gilbert pest control companies must be licensed by the Arizona Office of Pest Management (OPM). Verify licensing at azda.gov/opm. Gilbert homeowners should confirm OPM licensing and general liability insurance before allowing any pest control company to treat their property.

What time of year is pest pressure highest in Gilbert?

Gilbert sees peak pest pressure in two windows. Spring (February-May) brings scorpion season and termite swarm season. Monsoon season (July-September) drives a second surge — cockroaches and roof rats enter homes during heavy rains and scorpion activity remains elevated through October. Starting quarterly pest control in late winter gives Gilbert homeowners barrier protection before both peak windows.
