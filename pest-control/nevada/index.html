About Nevada pest control

Nevada's desert climate creates year-round pest pressure with no true dormancy season. Las Vegas and the surrounding metro sit firmly within bark scorpion territory and are classified as a heavy termite activity zone. The state's extreme heat — regularly exceeding 115 degrees in summer — does not eliminate pest activity but changes pest behavior, driving scorpions, cockroaches, and roof rats to seek shelter inside homes during peak daytime hours.

Las Vegas's massive tourism and hospitality industry also creates one of the highest bed bug introduction rates of any metro in the United States. Henderson's rapid expansion into Black Mountain terrain places newer neighborhoods directly at the desert interface, where bark scorpion populations are well established in undisturbed Mojave Desert soil. North Las Vegas's older housing stock and adjacency to Nellis AFB's undeveloped buffer land creates a pest profile heavier on rodents and cockroaches than the newer suburbs.

Nevada Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Nevada?

Nevada pest control costs $75-225 for a standard one-time treatment. Scorpion barrier treatment — the most common service in the Las Vegas metro — runs $100-250 initially with quarterly follow-ups at $75-150. Termite treatment ranges $500-2,500. Bed bug treatment runs $300-1,500 depending on the method and extent of infestation. Nevada's competitive pest control market means pricing varies significantly — getting multiple quotes is recommended before choosing a provider.

Does Nevada require pest control licensing?

Yes. All structural pest control companies in Nevada must be licensed by the Nevada Department of Agriculture Structural Pest Control Division. Verify any company's license at agri.nv.gov/pestcontrol before hiring. Always confirm licensing and liability insurance before allowing any treatment on your property.

Are bark scorpions in Nevada dangerous?

Bark scorpions are the only scorpion species in the United States considered medically significant. Nevada is within their native desert range. Their sting causes intense pain and can cause more serious effects in young children and elderly adults. If stung contact Nevada Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222. Professional quarterly barrier treatment significantly reduces scorpion activity around your home.
