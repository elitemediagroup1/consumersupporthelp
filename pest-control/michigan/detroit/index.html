Why Detroit Has a Serious Pest Problem

According to Orkin's most recent annual rankings, Detroit is the #3 bed bug city in America for 2025 in the United States. Detroit's older urban housing stock — much of it built before 1950 — combined with thousands of vacant properties creates intense rodent pressure across neighborhoods like Midtown, Corktown, and Hamtramck. Bed bugs spread quickly through the city's multi-unit rentals, German cockroaches thrive in older apartment buildings, and stink bugs and carpenter ants invade detached single-family homes every spring.

Michigan licenses commercial pest control technicians and businesses through the Michigan Department of Agriculture and Rural Development (MDARD). When you hire a Detroit exterminator, ask for their MDARD license number, certified applicator credential, and proof of liability insurance. A written treatment plan and re-service guarantee should always be included in the quote.

Detroit Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Detroit?

General pest control in Detroit ranges from $100 to $280 per treatment, with monthly maintenance plans averaging $30 to $55. Bed bug treatment runs $750 to $2,000 depending on home size and method, while termite control averages $450 to $1,800. Detroit homeowners typically get two to three written estimates before choosing — and reputable exterminators provide those quotes free after an in-home inspection.

Why is Detroit ranked #3 for bed bugs?

Orkin's 2025 rankings place Detroit at #3 nationwide for bed bug activity. High rental density, multi-family housing, and a large transient population contribute to the spread, and many older apartment buildings share HVAC and wall voids that let bed bugs move between units. Acting within the first week of noticing bites is the single biggest factor in a successful Detroit bed bug treatment.

Does Michigan require pest control companies to be licensed?

Yes. MDARD (Michigan Department of Agriculture and Rural Development) licenses every commercial pest control business and certified applicator in Michigan. Technicians must be certified in their specific category — 7A for structural work — and businesses must carry liability insurance. Always verify your Detroit exterminator's MDARD license number before signing a contract.

Why does Detroit have so many rats and mice?

Detroit's combination of older housing stock, vacant properties, and aging sewer infrastructure creates significant rodent pressure, especially in Downtown, Midtown, and Hamtramck. Norway rats nest in burrows around vacant lots and travel along utility lines into occupied homes. A proper Detroit rodent treatment always starts with an entry-point inspection — sealing gaps the diameter of a pencil — followed by baiting and exclusion work.

When should I schedule pest control in Detroit?

Early spring (March–April) is the best time for preventative treatments in Detroit because overwintering insects emerge and rodents look for new nesting sites. Termite swarms typically appear in April and May after warm rains. Bed bugs and German cockroaches breed indoors year-round, so address those infestations immediately rather than waiting for a season change.
