Michigan Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Michigan?

General pest control in Michigan runs $100 to $300 per treatment depending on city and home size. Bed bug treatment ranges $750 to $2,200, and termite control averages $450 to $2,000. Detroit and the surrounding metro tend to run slightly lower than Ann Arbor and Grand Rapids, but every licensed Michigan pest pro should offer a free inspection.

Who licenses pest control companies in Michigan?

The Michigan Department of Agriculture and Rural Development (MDARD) licenses every commercial pest control business and certified applicator in Michigan. Technicians must be certified in their applicable category — 7A is the standard structural-pest credential — and businesses must carry liability insurance. Verify any Michigan exterminator's license through MDARD before signing a contract.

What are the most common pests in Michigan?

Bed bugs, Norway rats, German cockroaches, carpenter ants, stink bugs, and house mice top the list. Carpenter ants are particularly common in Michigan's many older wood-frame homes, and rodent pressure is elevated in Detroit, Flint, and other older urban areas with vacant properties.

When should I schedule pest control in Michigan?

Early spring (March–April) is the best time for preventative pest control in Michigan because overwintering insects emerge and rodents look for new nesting sites. Termite swarms appear in April and May. Bed bugs and German cockroaches breed indoors year-round, so treat those infestations immediately.
