Why Columbus Has a Serious Pest Problem

According to Orkin's most recent annual rankings, Columbus is the #8 bed bug city in America for 2025 in the United States. Columbus is one of the fastest-growing cities in the Midwest, with a mix of historic neighborhoods like German Village and Bexley, dense apartments around Ohio State, and rapid new construction in Dublin and Westerville. That mix creates a varied pest pressure: bed bugs in student housing and short-term rentals, subterranean termites in older wood-frame homes, carpenter ants in damp foundations, and rodents pushed indoors by suburban edge development.

Ohio licenses commercial pest control applicators through the Ohio Department of Agriculture (ODA), Pesticide Regulation section. Before hiring a Columbus exterminator, ask for their ODA license number, written estimate, proof of liability insurance, and a written re-service guarantee. Columbus Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Columbus?

General pest control in Columbus runs about $100 to $275 per treatment, with quarterly maintenance plans averaging $30 to $55 per month. Bed bug treatment ranges $700 to $1,900 depending on home size and method, and termite work averages $400 to $1,800. Plan to compare two or three written estimates from licensed Columbus exterminators — most offer free in-home inspections.

Why is Columbus ranked #8 for bed bugs?

Orkin's 2025 rankings place Columbus at #8 nationwide. Heavy student turnover around Ohio State, a growing population of short-term rentals, and hotel traffic from conventions and Buckeyes football all contribute. Bed bugs spread through luggage and used furniture, and Columbus's mix of older and new multi-family housing gives them plenty of opportunities to relocate.

Does Ohio require pest control companies to be licensed?

Yes. The Ohio Department of Agriculture (ODA) licenses all commercial pesticide applicators and pest control businesses statewide, including in Columbus. Companies must hold the appropriate category license (10A or 10B for structural work), carry liability insurance, and employ certified applicators. Verify any Columbus exterminator's ODA credential before signing a contract.

Are termites a big problem in Columbus?

Yes. Subterranean termites are common throughout central Ohio, and Columbus sits in a moderate-to-heavy infestation zone. Older wood-frame homes in German Village, Clintonville, and Bexley are most at risk. A termite inspection every one to two years — combined with either a liquid barrier or in-ground bait station system — is standard for Columbus homeowners.

When should I schedule pest control in Columbus?

Early spring (March–May) is the best time for preventative pest control in Columbus because overwintering insects emerge and termite swarms appear after the first warm rains. Mosquito treatments are most effective April through October. Bed bugs and German cockroaches breed indoors year-round, so treat those infestations immediately rather than waiting for a particular season.
