Why Cincinnati Sits in a Moderate to Heavy Termite Zone

Cincinnati sits at the confluence of the Ohio River and Little Miami River, placing it in a moderate to heavy termite activity zone. Eastern subterranean termites are found throughout Hamilton County and are particularly active in older neighborhoods with mature landscaping and soil-to-wood contact. Older urban properties in Over-the-Rhine and historic neighborhoods see persistent American and German cockroach pressure, while summer mosquito activity is significant near the Ohio River and Little Miami River corridors.

All pest control companies operating in Cincinnati and Hamilton County must be licensed by the Ohio Department of Agriculture (ODA). Verify any company's Commercial Pesticide Applicator License at agri.ohio.gov before hiring. Hamilton County also has additional local regulations for pesticide use near the Ohio River and its tributaries. Always ask for proof of liability insurance and a written treatment plan with a re-service guarantee.

What is the best time for pest control in Cincinnati?

Spring (April-June) is Cincinnati's most critical pest control window — termite swarm season peaks in April and May along the Ohio River valley. Summer (June-August) brings peak mosquito pressure near the river corridors. Fall (September-November) is the key window for rodent exclusion before mice enter homes for winter. Cincinnati's warmer Ohio River valley climate means termites stay active later into fall than northern Ohio cities — year-round termite monitoring is recommended.
