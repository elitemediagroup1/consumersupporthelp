What pests are most common in Fort Wayne?

Fort Wayne homeowners most frequently encounter bed bugs in older apartment buildings especially near IPFW campus and the downtown corridor, eastern subterranean termites throughout Allen County particularly in older neighborhoods with soil-to-wood contact, mice and rats entering homes in fall and winter, and carpenter ants in areas with mature tree canopy especially in Aboite and southwest Allen County.

Does Indiana require pest control licensing in Fort Wayne?

Yes. All pest control companies in Fort Wayne and Allen County must be licensed by the Indiana State Chemist (OISC). Technicians must hold a valid Commercial Pesticide Applicator License. Verify licensing at oisc.purdue.edu before hiring. Allen County does not have additional local licensing beyond the state OISC requirement.

What is the best time for pest control in Fort Wayne?

Fall (September-October) is Fort Wayne's most critical pest window — mice begin entering homes as Indiana temperatures drop and bed bug season winds down from summer peaks. Spring (April-June) is termite swarm season and carpenter ant season in Allen County. Summer brings bed bug peaks with travel season and IPFW student housing turnover. Year-round quarterly plans are recommended for Fort Wayne homeowners with older housing.
