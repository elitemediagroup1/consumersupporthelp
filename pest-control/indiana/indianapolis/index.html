Why Indianapolis Has a Serious Pest Problem

According to Orkin's most recent annual rankings, Indianapolis is the #5 bed bug city in America for 2025 in the United States. Indianapolis pairs a dense urban core in Downtown, Broad Ripple, and Fountain Square with sprawling suburban subdivisions in places like Lawrence and Speedway. That mix creates a broad pest profile: bed bugs in apartments and hotels, subterranean termites in older wood-frame homes, and seasonal mosquito and ant pressure that builds from late spring through fall.

In Indiana, pest control licensing is administered by the Office of the Indiana State Chemist (OISC) at Purdue University. Commercial applicators must pass category exams (typically 7A for structural work) and businesses must carry liability insurance. Ask any Indianapolis exterminator for their OISC license number and a written estimate before approving treatment.

Indianapolis Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Indianapolis?

General pest control in Indianapolis runs $100 to $275 per treatment, and quarterly maintenance plans average $30 to $55 per month. Bed bug treatment ranges $750 to $2,000 depending on whether the pro uses heat or chemical methods, and termite work runs $400 to $1,800. Most licensed Indianapolis exterminators offer free in-home inspections, so plan to compare two or three written quotes before committing.

Why is Indianapolis ranked #5 for bed bugs?

Orkin's 2025 list places Indianapolis at #5 nationwide. Convention traffic at the Indiana Convention Center, hotel turnover, multi-family rentals, and college student housing around Butler and IUPUI all contribute. Bed bugs spread through luggage, used furniture, and shared laundry, and Indianapolis's mix of older apartments and dorms gives them plenty of opportunity to move between units.

Does Indiana require pest control technicians to be licensed?

Yes. The Office of the Indiana State Chemist (OISC) — housed at Purdue University — licenses every commercial pesticide applicator and pest control business in the state. Technicians must be certified in their applicable category, and businesses must carry liability insurance. Verify any Indianapolis exterminator's OISC credential before signing.

Are termites a big problem in Indianapolis?

Yes. Subterranean termites are active across central Indiana, and Indianapolis sits squarely in the moderate-to-heavy infestation zone. Older wood-frame homes in neighborhoods like Irvington, Meridian-Kessler, and Broad Ripple are most at risk. A termite inspection every one to two years — and a treatment plan that includes either liquid barrier or in-ground bait stations — is standard practice for Indianapolis homeowners.

When is the best time for pest control in Indianapolis?

Early spring (March–May) is ideal for preventative pest control in Indianapolis because overwintering insects become active and termite swarms appear after the first warm rains. Mosquito treatments are most effective from April through October. Bed bugs and German cockroaches breed indoors year-round, so don't wait — treat as soon as you see signs.
