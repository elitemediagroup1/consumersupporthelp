Does Virginia require pest control licensing?

Yes. All pest control companies in Virginia must be licensed by the Virginia Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (VDACS). Pesticide applicators must hold a Registered Pesticide Business License and appropriate applicator certification. Verify licensing at vdacs.virginia.gov before hiring.
