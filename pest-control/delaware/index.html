About pest control in Delaware

Delaware may be the second smallest state in the country but its pest pressure is anything but small. Delaware's position between Maryland and New Jersey — with Delaware Bay to the east and Chesapeake Bay drainage to the west — creates significant mosquito, termite, and tick pressure year-round. The state's Mid-Atlantic climate brings genuine cold winters that slow pest activity but the mild springs and humid summers keep Delaware in a moderate to heavy termite activity zone. The Brown Marmorated Stink Bug is one of Delaware's most distinctive seasonal pests — fall invasions into homes are a well-documented annual event throughout the state.

Delaware pest control costs $70-200 for a standard one-time treatment. Termite treatment ranges $475-2,300. Stink bug exclusion work runs $175-400 depending on home size and entry points. Does Delaware require pest control licensing?

Yes. All structural pest control companies in Delaware must be licensed by the Delaware Department of Agriculture Pesticides Section. Verify any company's license at dda.delaware.gov/pesticides/index.shtml before hiring. Always confirm licensing and liability insurance before allowing any treatment on your property.

What makes Delaware pest problems unique?

Delaware's small size means the entire state sits within easy reach of the Chesapeake Bay and Delaware Bay coastal ecosystems that drive mosquito and tick pressure. The Brown Marmorated Stink Bug is a particularly significant Delaware pest — the state sits in the core of the stink bug's established mid-Atlantic range and fall invasions affect virtually every home in the state annually. Delaware's proximity to the Philadelphia and Baltimore metro corridors means bed bug introduction pressure is higher than its small population would suggest — both airport corridors drive introduction into Delaware's rental market.
