Baltimore Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Baltimore?

Baltimore pest control costs $125-325 for a standard one-time treatment. Bed bug heat treatment averages $850-2,300 for a typical Baltimore row home or apartment. Termite treatment runs $500-2,100. Like Philadelphia, Baltimore's dense row home neighborhoods mean infestations spread quickly between attached properties, making early treatment essential.

Why does Baltimore rank in the top 15 for bed bugs?

Baltimore consistently ranks in Orkin's national top 15 for bed bug infestations. Baltimore's large stock of older row homes, high rental density in neighborhoods like Charles Village, Hampden, and East Baltimore, and active tourism and business travel through BWI Airport all contribute to elevated bed bug activity. Multi-unit attached housing is particularly vulnerable.

Does Maryland require pest control companies to be licensed?

Yes. All pest control companies in Maryland must be licensed by the Maryland Department of Agriculture (MDA). Commercial pesticide applicators must hold a valid MDA license in the appropriate pest control category. Verify any company's license at mda.maryland.gov before hiring.

What pests are most common in Baltimore homes?

Baltimore homeowners most frequently encounter bed bugs in older apartment buildings and row homes, mice and rats particularly in East Baltimore and older neighborhoods near the waterfront, German cockroaches in multi-unit properties, termites throughout Baltimore County and the city proper, and mosquitoes near the Patapsco River and Chesapeake Bay tributaries in summer.

What is the best time for pest control in Baltimore?

Spring (April-June) is termite swarm season and mosquito prevention season for Baltimore. Summer (June-August) is peak mosquito and bed bug season. Fall (September-November) is the critical window for rodent exclusion before mice seek winter shelter in Baltimore's older row homes. Year-round quarterly pest control is recommended given Baltimore's diverse and persistent pest pressure.
