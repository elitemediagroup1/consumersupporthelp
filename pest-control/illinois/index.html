Illinois Pest Control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Illinois?

Pest control in Illinois ranges from $100 to $350 per treatment for general pest service, with bed bug treatment running $800 to $2,500 and termite work $500 to $2,500 depending on home size. Costs run higher in Chicago and the collar counties than in downstate Illinois, but every licensed exterminator should offer a free in-home inspection and a written estimate.

Who licenses pest control companies in Illinois?

The Illinois Department of Public Health (IDPH) licenses all structural pest control businesses and technicians under the Illinois Structural Pest Control Act. Companies must carry liability insurance, and certified applicators are required for any restricted-use pesticide application. You can verify an Illinois exterminator's license number through the IDPH website.

What pests are most common in Illinois?

Bed bugs, German cockroaches, Norway rats, house mice, carpenter ants, pavement ants, and subterranean termites are the most common pests across Illinois. Chicago and other northern Illinois cities see the heaviest bed bug pressure, while termites and carpenter ants are spread fairly evenly statewide.

When is the best time of year for pest control in Illinois?

Early spring (March–May) is the ideal time for preventative pest control in Illinois because overwintering insects become active and rodents seek new nesting sites. Termite swarms appear in April and May after the first warm rains. Bed bugs and cockroaches breed year-round indoors, so address those infestations immediately.
