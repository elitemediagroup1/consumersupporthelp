What’s Available

Home security options for every home.

Professional Monitoring

A 24/7 monitoring center watches your system and dispatches police, fire, or medical responders when your alarm triggers — even when you cannot answer your phone. Typically $20–60 per month.

DIY Self-Monitoring

Smartphone alerts notify you when sensors trigger. No monthly fee beyond equipment cost. You are responsible for calling for help. Best for tech-comfortable buyers in lower-crime areas.

Professional Installation

A technician installs and configures your entire system. Ideal for larger homes, complex layouts, or buyers who want setup handled completely. Often included with monitoring agreements.

Wireless DIY Systems

Plug-and-play sensors and cameras that install without drilling or wiring. Most systems take under an hour to set up and are fully portable for renters.

Video Doorbells and Cameras

Standalone camera systems and video doorbells provide visibility without a full security package. Can be added to most existing systems or used independently.

Smart Lock Integration

Keyless entry, remote lock control, and access logs let you manage who enters your home from anywhere. Most integrate with major smart home platforms and security systems.

Save On Home Insurance

How a security system can reduce your home insurance premium.

Most homeowners insurance carriers offer premium discounts of 5 to 20 percent for homes with monitored security systems. For a homeowner paying $1,500 per year in insurance, a 10 percent discount saves $150 annually — enough to cover 3–6 months of basic monitoring costs.

Discounts vary by carrier and system type. Professional monitoring with a central station certificate typically qualifies for the largest discounts. Some carriers also offer discounts for standalone smoke detectors, water leak sensors, and smart locks even without a full security package.

Ask your insurance provider what documentation they need to apply the discount. Most require a monitoring certificate from your security provider which your installer can provide.

Common Questions

Home security questions, answered honestly.

How much does home security cost per month?

Monthly costs depend on whether you choose professional monitoring and which provider you select. Basic self-monitoring systems have no monthly fee beyond equipment cost. Professional monitoring typically runs $10–60 per month depending on the provider and plan features. Many providers offer free equipment when you sign a 2–3 year monitoring agreement which can significantly reduce upfront costs. Getting quotes from multiple providers is the best way to find the right price for your specific situation.

Can renters install a security system?

Yes. Most modern wireless security systems are designed for renters — they use adhesive mounts instead of screws, require no permanent wiring, and are fully portable when you move. Many renters install complete systems including door sensors, motion detectors, and cameras without any modifications to the property. Always check your lease for any restrictions before installing anything, even adhesive-mounted equipment.

Is a contract required for home security?

Not always. Many providers offer month-to-month monitoring with no long-term commitment, though the monthly rate is typically higher than contract pricing. Providers that offer free equipment usually require a 2–3 year contract to offset equipment costs. If you prefer flexibility, tell the specialist upfront — there are options at multiple price points with and without contracts.

What is the difference between a camera system and a full security system?

A camera system provides video surveillance and recording but does not typically include door and window sensors, motion detectors, or professional monitoring. A full security system combines cameras with sensors throughout the home and connects to a monitoring center that can dispatch emergency responders. Camera-only systems are a good starting point but leave gaps in coverage that a full system addresses.

How quickly do security systems alert police after an alarm?

With professional monitoring, the monitoring center is typically notified within seconds of an alarm trigger. After attempting to contact the homeowner (usually 30–60 seconds), they dispatch emergency services if they cannot confirm it is a false alarm. Response time after dispatch depends on local law enforcement. Self-monitored systems notify only your smartphone — response depends entirely on how quickly you see and respond to the alert.

Does home security work during a power outage?

Most professional security systems include battery backup that keeps the system running for several hours during a power outage. Systems with cellular communication continue to work even if your internet is down. Wi-Fi-only cameras and doorbells stop working during internet or power outages. If power-outage protection is a priority ask about cellular backup and battery life specifically.

What is a smart home security system?

Smart home security systems integrate with voice assistants, smart locks, thermostats, and other connected devices. They can be controlled through a single app and automated based on your schedule — for example automatically arming when you leave and disarming when you arrive. Most major security providers now offer smart home integration at various price points.

How do I choose between professional and DIY installation?

Professional installation makes sense for larger homes, complex layouts, older properties where placement optimization matters, or buyers who want the setup handled entirely by someone else. DIY installation works well for renters, budget-conscious buyers, and tech-comfortable homeowners with smaller spaces. Many wireless systems are designed for DIY installation and include detailed setup guides — most people complete setup in under two hours.

Does home security reduce the chance of a break-in?

Research consistently shows that visible security systems deter burglars. Studies indicate homes without security systems are significantly more likely to be targeted than homes with visible cameras and monitoring signs. The deterrent effect is most significant for opportunistic break-ins — which represent the majority of residential burglaries. Professional monitoring adds a response layer beyond deterrence.

What should I ask when getting a home security quote?

Key questions include: What equipment is included and what costs extra? Is professional installation included or additional? What is the contract length and early termination fee? What happens to my equipment if I cancel? Does the system use cellular or Wi-Fi communication? What is the monthly monitoring fee and what does it include? Is there a mobile app and what features does it have? Getting clear answers to these questions helps you compare quotes accurately.
