About Home Security

What home security covers

Modern systems combine door and window sensors, motion detectors, indoor and outdoor cameras, smart locks, and 24/7 professional monitoring that alerts emergency responders.

Who can qualify

Both homeowners and renters can install most modern systems. Wireless equipment makes installation easy without permanent modifications.

Choosing a Home Security System

Home break-ins happen roughly every 25 seconds in the U.S., and homes without security systems are up to three times more likely to be targeted. A modern system is no longer just an alarm box on the wall — it is a connected ecosystem of cameras, sensors, and smart devices that let you protect your family from anywhere.

Key components

  • Control panel or hub — the brain of the system
  • Door and window sensors — alert when entry points open
  • Motion sensors — detect movement inside or around the home
  • Indoor and outdoor cameras — record footage and provide live view
  • Video doorbells — see and speak with visitors at the door
  • Smart locks — keyless entry and remote lock control
  • Glass break, smoke, and CO sensors — protection beyond burglary

Professional vs self-monitoring

Professional monitoring (typically $20 to $60 a month) means a 24/7 monitoring center is alerted when your alarm triggers — they can dispatch police, fire, or medical responders even when you cannot answer your phone. Self-monitoring uses smartphone alerts only and is free with most systems but relies on you to call for help.

Wired vs wireless

Wired systems are reliable but require professional installation and may not be ideal for renters. Wireless systems are easy to install, portable, and dominate the modern market — many can be installed in under an hour.

How much does it cost?

Equipment ranges from about $200 for a basic DIY kit to $1,500 or more for a fully professionally installed system. Monthly monitoring averages $20 to $60. Many providers offer free equipment with a multi-year monitoring agreement.

Insurance discounts

Most homeowners insurance carriers offer 5 to 20 percent discounts for monitored security systems, which can offset much of the monthly monitoring fee over time.
