Your CCPA Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the rights to know, delete, and opt-out of certain data sharing.

Your Rights

  • Right to Know: Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information, subject to exemptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of certain data sharing/sale (we do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA).
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

How to Exercise

Email privacy@consumersupportnetwork.com with “CCPA Request” in the subject. We may need to verify your identity.

More Details

See our Privacy Policy for information about categories of data we collect and how we use/share information.
