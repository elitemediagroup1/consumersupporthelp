Common questions

What is final expense insurance?

Final expense (also called burial insurance) is a type of whole life insurance with a smaller benefit amount, designed to help cover funeral costs, medical bills, and other end-of-life expenses.

Who can qualify?

Many adults between roughly 50 and 80 may qualify for a simplified-issue policy. Eligibility, coverage amounts, and rates depend on your age, health, and state.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many plans do not require a medical exam. You'll usually answer a few health questions instead. A licensed agent can tell you which plans are available to you.

Who will I be speaking with?

Your call will be answered by a licensed insurance agent who can discuss available coverage. Consumer Support Help is not an insurance company and does not issue policies.
