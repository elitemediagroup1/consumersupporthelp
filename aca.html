About the Affordable Care Act

What is the ACA?

The Affordable Care Act, also known as Obamacare, is a federal law designed to make health coverage more accessible and affordable for individuals and families.

Who can enroll?

Most U.S. residents under 65 who are not covered by Medicare, Medicaid, or another qualifying plan may be eligible to enroll in an ACA marketplace plan.
