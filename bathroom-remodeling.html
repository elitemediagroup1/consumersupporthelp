Planning a Bathroom Remodel

A bathroom remodel is one of the highest-return home improvements you can make — both in everyday quality of life and at resale. According to industry surveys, midrange bathroom remodels typically recoup 60 to 70 percent of their cost at resale, while smaller updates like walk-in showers and new vanities often pay back even more.

Popular project types

  • Walk-in tub installs — designed for safety and aging in place
  • Tub-to-shower conversions — usually completed in 1 to 2 days
  • Curbless / barrier-free showers — modern and accessible
  • Vanity and countertop replacements
  • Tile and flooring upgrades
  • Full bathroom gut remodels

Average costs

A simple shower or tub replacement runs $5,000 to $10,000. A midrange full bathroom remodel typically costs $15,000 to $35,000. High-end primary bathroom remodels can exceed $50,000 depending on materials and layout changes.

Timeline expectations

A simple tub or shower install can be done in a single day. A standard full remodel takes 2 to 4 weeks. Custom layouts that involve moving plumbing or walls can run 4 to 8 weeks.

Aging in place and safety

Walk-in tubs, grab bars, comfort-height toilets, and curbless showers are popular upgrades for seniors and anyone with mobility concerns. Many remodelers specialize in ADA-compliant designs and may offer financing tailored to fixed incomes.

What to ask your contractor

  • Are you licensed and fully insured in this state?
  • Will you pull permits, and is that included in the price?
  • What is the projected timeline and daily schedule?
  • How are change orders handled?
  • What warranty do you offer on labor and materials?
