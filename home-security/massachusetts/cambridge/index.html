Cambridge Home Security FAQ

What security options work for Harvard and MIT students?

Harvard and MIT students living off-campus need portable wireless systems that work within lease restrictions. Wireless systems using adhesive mounts install without permanent modifications and disassemble at lease end. Basic door sensors, one indoor camera, and a video doorbell provide meaningful coverage for a typical Cambridge student apartment. Smart locks with rotating codes are useful for shared student housing common in Cambridge.

Is package theft really a problem in Cambridge?

Cambridge has lower overall property crime than other Greater Boston neighborhoods, but package theft remains a concern given high-value technology and academic deliveries that are frequently left at doors and in apartment building lobbies. Video doorbells, porch cameras, building lobby cameras for multi-unit buildings, and smart locks with in-door delivery for compatible carriers are the most effective deterrents.
