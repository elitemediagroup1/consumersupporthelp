Sacramento Home Security FAQ

What Sacramento neighborhoods have the highest crime rates?

Sacramento has variation by neighborhood. Some sections of south and north Sacramento have elevated property crime rates. Neighborhoods including East Sacramento, Land Park, Pocket-Greenhaven, and Curtis Park have lower rates. Surrounding suburbs including Elk Grove, Roseville, Folsom, Rancho Cordova, and Granite Bay have substantially lower property crime rates than the city.

Does Sacramento heat affect outdoor security cameras?

Yes. Sacramento summers regularly exceed 100°F with prolonged heat periods. Outdoor cameras must be rated for sustained operation at high temperatures. Camera housings should be shaded or positioned under eaves. Ask your installer about heat performance ratings — consumer-grade cameras designed for milder climates may fail prematurely in Sacramento's sustained summer heat.

What security options work for Sacramento state government workers?

Sacramento's state government workforce has high demand for portable wireless systems. A wireless system installs without permanent modifications in hours and can move to your next assignment. Look for month-to-month monitoring contracts that do not lock you in at a specific address. Smart locks with app-managed codes are practical for government workers with rotating schedules.

Does Sacramento require alarm permits?

Yes. The City of Sacramento and Sacramento County municipalities have alarm permit requirements for monitored security systems. Licensed installers handle permit registration as part of installation. Ask your installer to confirm Sacramento-specific requirements before your system is activated.
