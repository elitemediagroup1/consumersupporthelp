What security setup works for Willow Glen and Almaden homes?

Willow Glen and Almaden are established San Jose single-family neighborhoods with traditional home security needs. A standard setup includes door sensors on all entry doors, ground floor window sensors, motion detectors inside, a video doorbell with porch camera, outdoor cameras covering driveway and rear yard, smart locks with geofencing, and 24/7 monitoring. Smart home integration is a near-universal request in these affluent neighborhoods.

Can I monitor my San Jose home during business travel?

Yes. Silicon Valley tech workers travel frequently for business. Cellular security systems with smartphone apps provide real-time remote monitoring from anywhere in the world. Receive instant alerts, view live camera feeds, and remotely arm or disarm your system from any time zone. Do San Jose homeowners need to worry about California camera privacy law?

Yes. California has strict camera privacy laws including CIPA. Cameras must be positioned to cover your own property only — not neighboring yards, windows, or areas where neighbors have reasonable privacy expectations. Licensed installers familiar with California requirements ensure compliant positioning. Inform neighbors about cameras facing shared areas as a courtesy.
