Erie Home Security FAQ

Is Erie a high crime city?

Erie has elevated property crime rates compared to Pennsylvania state averages. Some sections of the city have higher rates while neighborhoods like Frontier and Glenwood Hills have lower rates. Surrounding Erie County suburbs including Millcreek Township and Harborcreek have substantially lower property crime rates than the city.

Do security cameras work in Erie winters?

Quality outdoor cameras are rated for sub-zero temperatures — Erie's lake-effect winters with extreme cold and heavy snow accumulation are within professional-grade camera operational ranges. However ice accumulation on lenses and freezing of sensor components are real concerns. Ask your installer about cold and snow performance ratings, weatherproof housings, and sensor placement that accounts for Erie's climate.

What security options work for Gannon University students?

Gannon University drives student rental demand near downtown Erie. Wireless portable systems are the right choice for student renters — adhesive-mounted sensors, indoor cameras, and video doorbells install without permanent modifications and move at lease end. Smart locks with rotating codes are useful for shared student housing.

Does Erie require alarm permits?

Yes. Erie and Erie County municipalities have alarm permit requirements for monitored security systems. Licensed installers handle permit registration as part of installation. Ask your installer to confirm Erie-specific requirements before your system is activated.

What about Lake Erie seasonal properties?

Lake Erie has significant seasonal property inventory along the lakeshore and on Presque Isle.
