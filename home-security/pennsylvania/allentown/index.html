Allentown Home Security FAQ

What Allentown neighborhoods have the highest crime rates?

Allentown's urban core has the highest property crime rates in the city. Some sections of the South Side and West End also have elevated rates. Surrounding Lehigh County suburbs including Allentown townships, Bethlehem Township, Macungie, and Emmaus have substantially lower crime rates than the city.

How does PPL Center revitalization affect Allentown security?

The PPL Center arena and surrounding downtown investment have brought new residential development and improved security infrastructure to the downtown corridor. Homeowners investing in downtown Allentown benefit from security systems that protect their investment. Many new downtown residential buildings have modern access control compatible with current smart security technology.

Does Allentown require alarm permits?

Allentown and Lehigh County municipalities have alarm permit requirements for monitored security systems. Requirements vary by municipality. Licensed installers handle permit registration as part of installation. Ask your installer to confirm Allentown-specific requirements before your system is activated.

What about Bethlehem and Easton home security?

Bethlehem and Easton are neighboring Lehigh Valley cities with similar but somewhat lower crime profiles than Allentown city core. Is home security worth it in Lehigh Valley suburbs?

Lehigh County and Northampton County suburbs have substantially lower crime rates than urban Allentown. What security setup works for downtown Allentown new buildings?

New downtown Allentown residential buildings near PPL Center have modern access control. Wireless sensor systems, smart locks with app control, and indoor cameras work well in modern buildings. Can I monitor my Allentown home remotely?

Yes. Cellular security systems with smartphone apps provide real-time remote monitoring from anywhere. Receive instant alerts for motion detection, door openings, or alarm triggers. View live camera feeds and remotely arm or disarm. Cellular systems work even if your home internet is disrupted.
