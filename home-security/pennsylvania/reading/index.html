How safe is Reading?

Reading has among the highest property crime rates in Pennsylvania. Some sections of the city have very elevated rates particularly in the downtown and northeast Reading areas. Why is professional monitoring important in Reading?

Professional 24/7 monitoring with rapid dispatch response is especially valuable in elevated-crime environments like Reading. When an alarm triggers, monitoring center operators can verify the event and dispatch police quickly. In high-crime areas this rapid response provides meaningful deterrence and protection.

Does Reading require alarm permits?

Yes. Reading and Berks County municipalities have alarm permit requirements for monitored systems. Licensed installers handle permit registration as part of the installation process. Ask your installer to confirm Reading-specific requirements before your system is activated.

What security setup works best in Reading?

Reading's elevated crime environment calls for full perimeter coverage with professional monitoring. A recommended setup includes door sensors on all entry doors, window sensors on accessible windows, motion detectors inside, a video doorbell, outdoor cameras covering driveway and rear yard, and 24/7 monitoring with rapid dispatch response.
