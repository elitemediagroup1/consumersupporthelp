Florida Home Security FAQ

Do Florida home security systems handle hurricanes?

Yes. Quality systems include cellular backup so monitoring stays active when Wi-Fi or power goes down. Battery backups typically last 24 hours, and equipment is rated for high humidity and salt air.

Are outdoor cameras rated for Florida heat and humidity?

Yes. Quality outdoor cameras are rated for 100+ degree heat and Gulf or Atlantic humidity. Look for IP65 or higher weather ratings.

How much does home security cost in Florida?

DIY self-monitored plans start around $10 to $20 per month. Professional monitoring typically runs $30 to $60 per month plus equipment.

Can I install a system in a Florida condo or rental?

Yes. Wireless peel-and-stick kits install without drilling and travel with you. Many Florida condo boards allow them without prior approval.

How fast can I get a Florida home security install?

DIY kits arrive in 2 to 5 days. Pro installs through partner providers are usually scheduled within a week across the major Florida metros.
