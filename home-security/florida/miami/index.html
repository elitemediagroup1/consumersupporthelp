Miami Home Security FAQ

Is home security worth it in Miami?

Yes. Miami property-crime rates run above the national average, especially in tourist-heavy areas. A monitored system with cameras deters break-ins and porch theft.

Do Miami systems handle hurricanes?

Yes. Quality systems include cellular backup so monitoring stays online when Wi-Fi or power goes down. Battery backups last 24 hours, and equipment is rated for salt air and humidity.

Can I install a system in a Miami condo?

Yes. Wireless peel-and-stick kits install without drilling, which matters in concrete condo towers. Many South Beach and Brickell condo boards allow them without prior approval.

What does home security cost in Miami?

DIY plans run $10 to $20 per month. Pro-monitored plans typically run $30 to $60 per month plus equipment.

Are outdoor cameras rated for Miami salt air?

Yes. Quality outdoor cameras are rated for coastal humidity and salt exposure. Look for IP65 or higher weather ratings.

How fast can I get installed in Miami?

DIY kits arrive in 2 to 5 days. Pro installs through partner providers are usually scheduled within a week across Miami-Dade.

Do I need a permit for a Miami alarm system?

Yes. Miami-Dade County requires alarm permits for monitored systems. Your provider will help you apply.

Can I monitor my Miami home from out of town?

Yes. Every recommended system includes a mobile app for live video, alerts, and remote arm/disarm — especially useful for snowbirds and vacation-home owners.
