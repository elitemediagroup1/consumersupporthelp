Buffalo Home Security FAQ

Is Buffalo a high crime city?

Buffalo has higher property crime rates than the New York State average. The East Side and parts of the West Side have the highest concentration of property crime in the city. Elmwood Village, Allentown, and North Buffalo have lower rates but are urban neighborhoods where security awareness is appropriate. Surrounding suburbs including Amherst, Cheektowaga, Tonawanda, Kenmore, and Williamsville have substantially lower property crime rates than the city.

Do security cameras work in Buffalo winters?

Quality outdoor cameras are rated for temperatures well below zero — Buffalo winters are within the operational range of professional-grade outdoor cameras. However heavy snow accumulation on camera lenses and ice affecting door and window sensors are real considerations. Ask your installer about weatherproofing, camera housing protection, and sensor placement that accounts for Buffalo's extreme ice and snow conditions.

What security options work for Buffalo rental properties near UB?

Buffalo has a significant rental market near the University at Buffalo North Campus in Amherst and the South Campus near Elmwood Village. Wireless systems allow easy access code management between tenants. Smart locks with rotating codes are popular with Buffalo landlords. Basic camera systems covering entry points are commonly installed in student rental properties. A specialist can recommend landlord-appropriate systems for the Buffalo student rental market.

Is home security worth it in Buffalo suburbs like Amherst or Cheektowaga?

Buffalo suburbs have significantly lower property crime rates than the city. What about seasonal homes near Chautauqua Lake?

Western New York has significant seasonal property inventory on Chautauqua Lake, Lake Erie, and in the Southern Tier. Cellular systems that do not rely on home internet are especially important for seasonal properties where internet service may be disconnected off-season.

Does Buffalo require alarm permits?

The City of Buffalo and surrounding municipalities have alarm permit requirements for monitored systems. Requirements vary by municipality. Licensed installers typically handle permit requirements as part of installation. Ask your installer to confirm local permit requirements for your specific address before your system is activated.
