Washington DC Home Security FAQ

Is home security worth it in Washington DC?

Yes. DC has dense neighborhoods with high foot traffic and frequent package deliveries. A monitored system with video doorbell and door sensors deters porch theft and break-ins.

Can I install a system in a DC rental or condo?

Most renters and condo owners use wireless, peel-and-stick equipment that installs without drilling. We match you with providers offering renter-friendly kits.

Do DC condo associations allow exterior cameras?

Many condo boards allow cameras inside your unit and at your front door, but exterior cameras on shared walls may require board approval. Check your bylaws.

What does home security cost in DC?

Self-monitored DIY plans start around $10 to $20 per month. Professionally monitored systems typically run $30 to $60 per month plus equipment.

How fast can I get a system installed in DC?

DIY kits arrive in 2 to 5 days and self-install in under an hour. Professional installs through partner providers are usually scheduled within a week.
