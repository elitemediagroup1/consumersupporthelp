What is included in a Virginia home security setup?

A typical package starts with a control panel and entry sensors on exterior doors, motion detectors, and 24/7 professional monitoring. Most Virginia homeowners add a video doorbell, two to four indoor or outdoor cameras, glass-break sensors, and smart locks. Renters often skip drilled installs and use peel-and-stick kits that travel between apartments.

If you have a detached garage, workshop, or guesthouse common in rural parts of the Commonwealth, ask about outdoor cameras with extended range, solar power, and cellular backup. We can match you with providers who handle multi-building properties.

Frequently asked questions about home security in Virginia

How much does a home security system cost in Virginia?

Most Virginia households spend $200 to $600 upfront for equipment and $25 to $55 per month for professional monitoring. Renters in Arlington or Alexandria often go DIY with no contract. Call (866) 753-7189 for free side-by-side quotes.

Do Virginia security systems work during hurricanes or power outages?

Yes — reputable systems include cellular backup so monitoring continues when internet or power goes down. This is especially important in Hampton Roads where storm-related outages are common. Ask about battery backup duration when you call.

Can I get a home security system as a renter in Virginia?

Absolutely. Wireless and peel-and-stick systems install without drilling and travel with you when you move. We match renters in Arlington, Alexandria, Norfolk, and across the Commonwealth with no-contract options.

Do I need a permit for an alarm system in Virginia?

Many Virginia municipalities require alarm permits to reduce false dispatches. Arlington, Alexandria, Richmond, Norfolk, and Virginia Beach all have permit programs. Your installer will walk you through the local rules during setup.

Are there homeowners insurance discounts for security systems in Virginia?

Yes. Most Virginia insurers offer 5% to 20% off premiums for monitored systems. Bring the monitoring certificate to your agent after installation to claim the discount.
