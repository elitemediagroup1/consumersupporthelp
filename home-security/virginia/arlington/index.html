System Options for Arlington Homes

Most Arlington households start with a control panel, door and window contacts on exterior openings, a couple of motion detectors, and 24/7 professional monitoring. From there, popular add-ons include video doorbells, two to four cameras (one front, one back, plus garage and side gate if you have them), glass-break sensors for big living-room windows, and smart locks. Condo dwellers and Rosslyn renters usually skip drilled installs and use peel-and-stick door and window sensors with a hub the size of a smoke alarm. If you have a detached garage or a basement entrance, ask about outdoor cameras with cellular backup so coverage continues if your home internet drops.
