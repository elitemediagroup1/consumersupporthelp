Do I need an alarm permit in Richmond?

Yes. The City of Richmond requires alarm registration and charges fees for repeated false alarms. Henrico and Chesterfield counties have similar programs. Your installer walks you through the application during setup.

Can I install a security system in a Fan or Church Hill historic home?

Yes. Wireless and peel-and-stick systems install without drilling into historic trim or masonry, which keeps you compliant with historic-district rules and protects original woodwork.

Do Richmond systems work during a power or internet outage?

Reputable systems include both battery backup and cellular communication so monitoring keeps working during summer thunderstorms or winter ice events that knock out power or internet.
