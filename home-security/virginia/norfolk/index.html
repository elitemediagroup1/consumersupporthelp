Do I need an alarm permit in Norfolk?

Yes. The City of Norfolk requires alarm registration and charges fees for repeated false alarms. Your installer walks you through the application during setup so dispatch happens properly.

Will my system work during a hurricane?

Reputable Norfolk systems include cellular backup so monitoring continues when power and internet both fail. Battery backup typically holds the panel for 24 hours or more. Ask about extended-life batteries when you call.

Can I get a system that handles flood risk?

Yes. Many providers offer water sensors for entry points, elevated control panel placement, and weatherproof exterior gear. Important for Ocean View, East Beach, and low-lying neighborhoods.

Can military families pause monitoring during deployment?
