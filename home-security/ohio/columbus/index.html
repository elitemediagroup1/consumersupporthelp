Do I need a permit for a home security alarm in Columbus?

Yes. The City of Columbus requires alarm system permits for monitored systems. Your specialist will walk you through the application and any annual fees.

Can I get a discount on my home insurance with a security system?

Most major Ohio insurers including State Farm, Allstate, Nationwide, and Erie offer 5% to 20% discounts for monitored systems. Ask your specialist for the documentation to claim it.

Are DIY security systems good for Columbus apartments?

Yes. SimpliSafe, Ring Alarm, and Abode are popular with Columbus apartment renters because they install without drilling, work with rental leases, and move easily when you relocate.

Do Columbus security systems work in cold winters?

Yes — quality outdoor cameras and sensors are rated for Ohio winter temperatures, and cellular backup keeps monitoring active during power outages from ice and snowstorms.
