Is Trenton safe?

Does Trenton require alarm permits?

Yes. Trenton and Mercer County municipalities have alarm permit requirements for monitored security systems. Licensed installers handle permit registration as part of installation. Ask your installer to confirm Trenton-specific requirements before your system is activated.

What security setup works best in elevated-crime Trenton neighborhoods?

Full perimeter coverage with professional monitoring is recommended for elevated-crime Trenton neighborhoods. A typical setup includes door sensors on all entry doors, ground floor window sensors, motion detectors inside, a video doorbell, outdoor cameras covering driveway and rear yard, and 24/7 monitoring with rapid dispatch response.
