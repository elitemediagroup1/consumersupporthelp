Houston Home Security FAQ

Is home security worth it in Houston?

Yes. Houston's property-crime rate runs above the national average, and hurricane-season power outages make cellular-backup monitoring especially valuable.

Do Houston systems handle hurricanes?

Quality systems include cellular backup so monitoring stays online when Wi-Fi or power goes down. Battery backups typically last 24 hours, and the equipment is rated for Gulf Coast humidity.

What does home security cost in Houston?

DIY self-monitored plans run $10 to $20 per month. Pro-monitored plans typically run $30 to $60 per month plus equipment, depending on cameras and smart-home add-ons.

Can I install a system in a Houston apartment?

Yes. Wireless peel-and-stick kits install without drilling and move with you. Many Houston renters use DIY systems in Midtown, Montrose, and Heights apartments.

How fast can I get installed in Houston?

DIY kits arrive in 2 to 5 days. Pro installs through partner providers are usually scheduled within a week across the Houston metro.

Do I need a permit for a Houston alarm system?

The City of Houston requires alarm permits for monitored systems. Your provider will walk you through the application — it's a one-time process.

Will outdoor cameras survive Houston heat?

Yes. Quality outdoor cameras are rated for 120-degree heat and Gulf Coast humidity. Look for IP65 or higher weather ratings.

Can I monitor my Houston home from out of town?

Yes. Every system we recommend includes a mobile app for live video, alerts, and arm/disarm from anywhere.
