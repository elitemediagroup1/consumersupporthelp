Coverage Types

What Pittsburgh homeowners should know.

A standard policy for your Pittsburgh home includes several protections. Here is what each covers locally.

Dwelling coverage

Pays to rebuild your Pittsburgh home at current local construction costs after a covered loss — set it to full replacement cost.

Personal property

Covers your belongings inside the home against theft and covered perils, important for Pittsburgh homeowners.

Liability protection

Protects you if a guest is injured at your Pittsburgh property; most homeowners carry at least $300,000.

Additional living expenses

Pays for temporary housing if your Pittsburgh home is uninhabitable after a covered event.

Medical payments

Covers minor guest medical bills regardless of fault, typically $1,000–$5,000.

Winter Storms & Flooding

Pennsylvania’s river valleys flood frequently and standard policies exclude flood, so homeowners near the Susquehanna, Schuylkill, or Allegheny should ask about a separate flood policy. Ice dams and burst pipes are common winter claims.
